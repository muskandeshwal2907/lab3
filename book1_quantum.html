Book Synopsis & Overview

The Quantum Enigma bridges the colossal chasm between the microscopic paradoxes of quantum electrodynamics and our everyday perception of physical reality. Authored by acclaimed astrophysicist Dr. Evelyn Reed, this groundbreaking work explores wave-particle duality, quantum entanglement, Bell's theorem, and the profound philosophical implications of the Copenhagen vs. Many-Worlds interpretations.

Written with lyrical lucidity and rigorous mathematical foundation, Reed guides both seasoned physicists and inquisitive general readers through the enigmatic frontiers of quantum cosmology, quantum computing, and the elusive search for a Unified Field Theory.
