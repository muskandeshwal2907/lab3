The Epic Tale & Synopsis

In a cosmos where celestial astrolabes govern interstellar warp lanes, the ancient gates constructed by the extinct Precursors have suddenly reawakened. Across three star systems, young archivist Aaron Vance uncovers a forbidden manuscript detailing a forgotten apocalyptic war between stellar monarchs and entities lurking inside the void between galaxies.

Lyra Thorne crafts an intoxicating blend of hard space exploration and mythic high fantasy, celebrated worldwide for its breathtaking world-building, richly layered characters, and intricate astrological magic system.
