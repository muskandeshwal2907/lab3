Botanical Dissertation & Overview

Celebrated by naturalists, botanists, and gardeners across the globe, The Secret Life of Ancient Flora is a sumptuous, illustrated treatise investigating four hundred million years of plant evolution. Dr. Alistair Finch spent four decades collecting rare herbarium specimens across Cloud Forests in Madagascar, Andean alpine plateaus, and ancient temperate rainforests of Tasmania.

Illustrated with over 160 archival copperplate engravings and botanical watercolors, Finch reveals the complex biochemical communication channels, distress pheromones, and subterranean nutrient highways that allow trees to nurture neighboring saplings across centuries.
