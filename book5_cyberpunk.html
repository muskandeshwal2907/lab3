Treatise Synopsis & Society

Cybernetic Dreams is an electrifying journey into the fast-approaching epoch of transhumanism, direct brain-computer interfaces (BCIs), synthetic telepathy, and sovereign digital consciousness. Elena Rostova investigates the collision between autonomous neuro-technologies and personal human agency.

Addressing both high-stakes ethical dilemmas and groundbreaking neurotechnological engineering, the book offers an unforgettable roadmap for navigating the merger of biological minds with artificial superintelligence.
